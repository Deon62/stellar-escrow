Lumenswags

Lumenswags is an on-chain escrow for buying and selling Stellar merchandise. As the buyer, you lock funds in a smart contract. The seller ships and adds proof. When you confirm delivery, funds are released to the seller — no middleman, fully transparent.

Connect your wallet to get started.

Requires Freighter or another Stellar wallet.
